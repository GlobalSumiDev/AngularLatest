The Headquarters for Enterprise Intelligence, One Home

Sumi Sense AI Home serves as a centralized enterprise hub where all AI agents are created, managed, and accessed from a single location. Department administrators can design and configure AI agents tailored to specific business functions, knowledge domains, and workflows, then assign them to relevant users across the organization. Each user gains access only to the agents assigned to them, ensuring a personalized and role-based experience. Through a simple chat interface, users can interact with these AI agents to retrieve organizational knowledge, receive guidance, automate routine activities, and complete work-related tasks efficiently. By bringing all enterprise AI agents together in one unified platform, Sumi Sense AI Home enhances productivity, streamlines knowledge access, and enables employees to leverage AI capabilities seamlessly in their daily work.

  • Centralized AI Agent Hub – Access all enterprise AI agents from a single, unified platform.
  • Department-Driven Agent Creation – Department administrators can create and configure AI agents tailored to specific business needs.
  • Role-Based Agent Assignment – Assign AI agents to users based on their roles, teams, or responsibilities.
  • Conversational User Experience – Interact with AI agents through an intuitive chat interface for quick assistance and support.
  • Enterprise Knowledge Access – Retrieve accurate department-specific and organizational knowledge instantly.
  • Task Automation and Execution – Enable AI agents to assist with routine tasks, workflows, and business processes.
  • Secure and Controlled Access – Ensure users only access the AI agents and information relevant to their responsibilities.
